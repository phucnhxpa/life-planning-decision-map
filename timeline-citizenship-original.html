Conditional decade strategy · review annually

What to do from 2026 to 2036

Derived from the three terminal goals and the 24 completed research reports.

Priority rule: protect legal, health, financial and deadline constraints → require a socially sustainable weekly life with protected French-learning time → maximise scarce technical capability and ownership → preserve options → buy prestige only for a verified mechanism → use citizenship, location, degrees and company structures as tools.
2026–2027

Protect the gates and create options

  • Make A-level and ESAT performance the dominant education priority.
  • Preserve income, lawful French status, tax/URSSAF compliance, health and sleep.
  • Build a month-level residence/permit/absence ledger; calculate separately the five-year pre-filing naturalisation clock, residence through decree signature, and the 60-qualifying-month LTR-UE clock; prepare B2/civic evidence.
  • Apply in parallel to Cambridge deferred 2028, strong UK 2027 routes and a strong French 2027 route.
  • Run one or two sequential, high-information trials across theory, computation and build/experimental work; complete the work-layer comparison before accepting an offer.
2027–2031/32

Build an evidence-chosen technical foundation

  • Default to the best verified 2027 route unless the exact Cambridge wait test passes.
  • Choose one hard-to-substitute depth from evidence, not identity; add computation/engineering and implementation competence.
  • If France is chosen, plan a complete Master-level route—not a Cambridge transfer.
  • Protect recurring social time and serious French-learning time; reject routes whose ordinary workload or commute makes both consistently unrealistic.
  • Build admissions assets plus direct goal evidence: owned work, one cross-domain transfer project, method/direction choices, and growing control over mission, team or resources.
  • Run French citizenship or LTR-UE in parallel as resilience, not as the definition of success.
2031/32 decision gate

Choose a moat, not a PhD label

  • Enter a PhD only for a named frontier bottleneck with proven mentoring, scarce tools/data and genuine ownership.
  • Require strong overlap between the lab's weekly work and the future technical critical path.
  • If a research-engineer or high-consequence R&D role produces more capability and faster system feedback, take it instead.
  • Build product, customer, regulatory and team skills in parallel whichever route you choose.
2032–2035

Accumulate founder advantage

  • Own increasingly complete systems and difficult failures.
  • Build a network of complementary specialists rather than trying to master every field personally.
  • Treat Europe-first as a revisable, moderate-confidence prior while its status, team, collaborator and live-funding advantages remain verified.
  • Use the UK and US through visits, collaborations, roles or a subsidiary; relocate for a named lab, customer, facility, team or financing trigger.
By trigger · possibly after 2036

Found only when the vehicle fits

  • Require a painful validated problem, credible technical or organisational advantage, a complementary team, and a financing/customer path.
  • Preserve the standing civilian-alignment and acceptable-control constraints.
  • If these conditions are absent, lead or join an aligned frontier organisation and continue accumulating founder advantage; do not force founding into the decade.
Every 12 months

Re-run the decision

  • Review on offers, fee/funding outcomes, a status milestone, a named lab/R&D role, a validated startup problem, or a health/financial shock.
  • Ask whether the current route is still producing scarce capability, ownership, relationships and option value.
Spend the decade building one evidence-chosen hard-to-substitute technical depth, computation/engineering competence, genuine research and system ownership, and demonstrated transfer across domains; preserve French/EU status when its remaining path is short and verified; choose education by capability production rather than prestige; use a PhD only for a specific moat; treat Europe-first as a revisable current prior; found only when a company is the best vehicle for a validated frontier problem—even if that occurs after 2036.
This is a conditional strategy, not a prediction or legal/admissions guarantee. The full synthesis is preserved in ten-year-strategy.md.

How to read the academic routes: Option D is the only route with the full French sequence: learn French through 2028 → complete the entire three-year French undergraduate degree in 2028–31 → complete the default two-year French physics master in 2031–33. Routes containing Cambridge undergraduate years instead proceed to a UK master lasting one or two years before the PhD. Do not attach the French master to a partial-Cambridge route. Five years is only a French-naturalisation eligibility-planning threshold, not guaranteed approval. The robust legal sequence remains genuinely France-based → apply in 2029 → remain France-centred during processing → signed decree → verify status → relocate; if the decree is unexpectedly still pending, remain France-centred rather than relocating. The long-term resident–EU card is an optional fallback, not a step before citizenship. The 0–3-year preparation range remains only on the 2027 non-Cambridge row. Option B’s Year-2 transfer still requires written confirmation from the exact Cambridge course and College. UK: Student years count toward ten-year Long Residence, but not the separate Global Talent or Skilled Worker settlement clocks. US: a PhD does not grant a Green Card. Every Green Card date is a planning assumption requiring a separately successful immigrant route—such as qualifying employer sponsorship, EB-1 or EB-2 NIW—and visa availability. The ordinary five-year naturalisation clock begins only with lawful permanent residence, not F-1, OPT, H-1B or O-1. Here, “bridge” means the temporary post-PhD period in a valid nonimmigrant work status while an independent Green Card route is pursued; “LPR” means lawful permanent resident—the legal status shown by a Green Card; citizenship comes only after N-400 approval and the oath.
